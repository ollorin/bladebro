## Concurrency
The getTargets call runs on the tick path with a 1 s cap, only when the list holds an uncached, not-at-start id, on the current source's own socket. It never holds the snap mutex or the hub lock. A finished/retiring source is never asked (no lookup happens and nothing is cached as Unknown for that tick).
